# Grading the status objective

The `status` fitness objective scores a candidate on three values: 0.0 when some component formula is unsatisfiable on its own, 0.5 when every component is satisfiable but the candidate is not realisable, and 1.0 when it is realisable and well-separated (`include/fitness/status.hpp:16-23`, computed in `src/fitness/status.cpp:12-27`). Three levels give a genetic algorithm (GA) almost no gradient to climb, and today all 21 corpus specifications sit at the single value 0.5. The question is how to grade the region below realisability. The answer this report reaches is a greedy *maximal realisable subset* (MRS) score over the conjunct-split guarantee side, which spreads those 21 specifications over 14 distinct values.

## The recovered counterstrategy work

An earlier attempt graded the same region by counterstrategy size, and only the session memory survives it. Branch `feat/counterstrategy-volume-stub` and worktree `.claude/worktrees/csv-classb` no longer exist, and commits 7883476, 3477557, f733009, 1572a4f, 54666ca and 5a65448 were collected by `git gc`, since no tag held them the way `provenance/tlsf-branch` holds the TLSF branch. The files `src/fitness/counterstrategy_volume.cpp`, `tools/counterstrategy_volume_probe.cpp` and `tools/counterstrategy_sweep.cpp` are absent from every ref, and the "REPORT.md" the memory cites never existed in this repository's history.

What the work established is the libspot game construction for SPOT 2.15.1: `ltl_to_game(f, sys_outputs)` un-negated, with the environment region read as `get_state_winners()[s] == false`. The dualisation recipe recorded in the missing REPORT.md was wrong, and made the environment win everywhere.

The measurements it produced were all negative. Region trace volume over horizons k = 2..20 came out anti-monotone on arbiter: 128 against 256 at k=2, and 3.4e10 against 1.1e12 at k=10, for 0 against 1 assumptions, with the normalised fraction degenerating to 1, 1, 0. Edge permissiveness collapsed the same way, giving 2 environment states and 0 losing moves at 0 assumptions (fraction 1.0), 3 and 0 at 1 assumption (1.0), and 6 and 4 at 2 assumptions, where the specification is realisable (0.238). A "class B" normalised fraction on the detector specification looked strictly monotone at horizon 10, running 1.000, 0.0954, 0.0160, 0.0074, 0, and an 8-specification sweep retracted it: f@10 was bit-identical across structurally unrelated specifications, 0.237305 at k=1 and 0.0953674 at k=2 for detector, rg2, rg1, simple-arbiter and prioritized alike. That is an artifact of mutual-exclusion assumptions shrinking the input space, not counterstrategy geometry.

## Why volume metrics fail structurally

A fresh probe run for this report reproduces the failure and explains it. The bounded environment-defeat fraction builds a nondeterministic automaton for the specification, existentially projects the output *atomic propositions* (APs) away with `autfilt --remove-ap`, counts the length-k input prefixes admitting some output word, and divides by 2^(|I|k). It returns exactly 1.000 at every horizon k = 1..8 for arbiter with 0, 1 and 2 fairness assumptions, and for the guarantee-dropping chain too. The projected automaton has 1 state, because every finite prefix extends. Prefix counting cannot see liveness.

The measure-theoretic version fails for a related reason. The input words that defeat a liveness-unrealisable specification, those where `F G !r0` holds, have measure zero under uniform inputs, since a random input word satisfies fairness almost surely. Any volume or counting metric is therefore blind to the family the search most needs help with, which is the arbiter family: the campaign data shows 100% zero-yield for `amba` (96/96) and `prioritized-arbiter` (240/240), 97.9% for `full-arbiter` (235/240), and 68.5% for `arbiter` (3448/5032). The earlier work found nothing because of a property of the metric class rather than of that implementation.

## What the literature offers

AuRUS, the model-counting repair search this project derives from, treats realisability as binary and scores candidates on syntactic plus model-counting semantic similarity alone. The current three-level objective is already ahead of that published baseline. Quantitative synthesis grades quality among implementations that already satisfy the specification, so it is undefined on the unrealisable side. Bounded synthesis orders realisable specifications by implementation size, and for an unrealisable specification every bound fails identically, leaving the parameter constant across the whole unrealisable region.

The gradient this report recommends comes instead from maximum realisability and from the unrealisable-core line of work. Maoz and Shalom find that most specifications have more than one core, which bears directly on how a greedy walk is expected to behave. Approximate implementations for unrealisable specifications value a specification by the fraction of bounded-size lassos on which it is satisfiable, which is the principled continuous option and the one worth trying if the recommendation below stalls. No paper was found that evaluates counterstrategy automaton size or winning-region volume as a distance-to-realisability signal, neither validating nor refuting it. `ltlsynt` emits no counterstrategy at all; `--print-game-hoa` and `--print-pg` dump the unsolved parity game.

## The recommendation, measured

Grade by the greedy MRS. Split the lowered guarantee side into top-level conjuncts, so that `G(a & b)` becomes `G a` and `G b`, which preserves the language, then walk the parts in specification order and keep each one whose addition leaves the accumulated subset realisable against the full, unchanged environment side. The score is |kept| / |parts|. It is 1.0 exactly when the specification is realisable, monotone under guarantee-set inclusion, and deterministic, which matters because this repository pins seed reproducibility.

On the assumption-strengthening chains where counterstrategy volume was anti-monotone or degenerate, the MRS score climbs. Arbiter, adding request-fairness assumptions, runs 0.600, 0.800, 1.000, against verdicts of unrealisable, unrealisable and realisable. Simple-arbiter with 3 clients runs 0.667, 0.778, 0.889, 1.000. Detector, adding the six pairwise input-mutex assumptions one at a time, is flat at 0.500 across all six unrealisable links when undecomposed, and runs 0.143, 0.286, 0.429, 0.571, 0.714, 0.857, 1.000 once conjunct-split. Conjunct splitting is not optional. Arbiter's chain and simple-arbiter's chain share no value, so the bit-identical artifact that retracted the class B result does not recur.

The corpus measurement covers all 21 `examples/*/spec.tlsf`, using the binary's own `combined LTL:` lowering from `build-release/ltl`. An earlier probe that rebuilt the implication from per-section output disagreed with `realize` on five specifications, because ASSERT rows print unwrapped, and that probe was discarded. All 21 verdicts agree with `realize` as unrealisable. Grade levels, counting parts plus one, run from a minimum of 3 through a median of 6 to a maximum of 17, and the 21 specifications take 14 distinct MRS values.

Cost, as the wall time of one greedy sweep against one whole-specification `ltlsynt --realizability` call, is 1.1x at minimum, 4.6x at the median and 12.6x at maximum. The ratio is lowest where the whole-specification check is dearest: `amba` costs 1470ms whole and 1665ms swept, a ratio of 1.1x over 13 parts, because every subset query is strictly easier than the full one. Population-level sharing cuts it further. Over a synthetic 60-candidate population of arbiter mutants, status-only issued 60 queries resolving to 48 `ltlsynt` execs, while MRS issued 300 queries resolving to 112 execs, for 2.33x the execs and 2.20x the wall time rather than the 5x the guarantee count suggests, because greedy prefixes recur across near-identical candidates and `RealizabilityChecker` already memoises by formula string.

Three implementation points follow. `extract_muc` (`include/tlsf/mucs.hpp`) already runs QuickXplain over the guarantee side against a fixed environment side and relies on the same monotonicity, so the machinery exists on the Temporal Logic Synthesis Format (TLSF) path and the FRETISH path needs the equivalent. An undecided (`nullopt`) subset query must resolve as unrealisable, matching how `status_score` already treats an unanswered satisfiability check at `src/fitness/status.cpp:18-22`. MRS subsumes the current scale rather than replacing its meaning, since 1.0 still requires the well-separation fold that both front ends apply.

## A defect found on the way

`crowding_distances` in `src/genetic/nsga2.cpp:102-111` assigns `k_infinity` to `front.front()` and `front.back()` before the `range <= 0.0` guard on the next line. A zero-range objective therefore still pins two front members above every finitely-scored member, and the `std::sort` at line 98 is not stable, so which two get pinned is an arbitrary permutation of the tied members. The header contract at `include/genetic/nsga2.hpp:38` and the inline comment at `src/genetic/nsga2.cpp:104-105` both describe behaviour the code does not implement. A coarse objective takes the worst of this: a continuous objective has unique extremes, whereas three values may leave hundreds of members tied at 0.5, making the two that receive infinite crowding distance selection noise. Grading the objective finitely reduces the exposure, and the guard should be moved regardless.

## How to decide it

The decision belongs in a campaign of the kind this project already runs: MRS against the current three-level score as a crossed factor over the corpus, reading yield and `implies_ideal`. The probes above tested MRS monotonicity along assumption-strengthening chains alone, and the GA walks no such chain. Its trajectories mutate guarantees, and whether the MRS score stays monotone along them is the one thing untested here.

The prior counterstrategy attempt failed on a property of its metric class rather than on effort, and it is worth keeping that in view when reading the MRS numbers, which are so far measurements on chains that a search does not traverse. What the campaign settles is whether a gradient that exists on paper is one the population can climb.
